FAQ

What is WeduLab?

WeduLab is an interactive teaching lab platform for the classroom. It currently offers 22 3D and animated labs covering Physics, Geography, Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics and more.

Do I need to install software or create an account?

No, and it is free. The labs run directly in the browser: open the page and start, whether projecting in class or exploring on your own.

Which grade levels is it for?

Mainly middle and high school classrooms; some chemistry labs extend to university organic and structural chemistry.

Which languages are supported?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish and English.
